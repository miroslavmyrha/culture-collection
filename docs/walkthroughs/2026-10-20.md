# Role walkthroughs, 2026-10-20

The daily work of each lab role, performed in the app and measured before the design pass
(#30). No curator from a real collection was available, so the tasks come from collection
practice (OECD Best Practice Guidelines for BRCs 2007, WFCC Guidelines 2010) and were walked
by the team.

## How it was measured
- 39 tasks: 10 curator, 10 technician, 10 manager, 9 viewer. Each is a Playwright script in
  `frontend/e2e/walkthroughs/` that works the way a person does: visible links and labels, the
  keyboard where natural, no typed URLs. Every task starts on Home, signed in.
- Steps are clicks, key presses, text entries (one per field) and screens scrolled to reach a
  control. Page loads are changes of the path. Requests are calls to `/api/`.
- Time (run) is what the run took against the production build and a local API. Time (person,
  est.) adds the keystroke-level model operators of Card, Moran and Newell (1980) for every
  action (decide 1.35 s, point 1.1 s, click 0.2 s, key 0.28 s, hand to keyboard 0.4 s) and
  1 s per screen scrolled. It is an estimate for a practised user, not an observation.
- Data: `seed_dev` on a fresh database, labs Bacteriology and Regulated agents, desktop
  viewport 1280 x 800, one run (44 of 44 passed). Rerun:
  `cd frontend && DEV_PASSWORD=... npm run walkthroughs`.

Result: 24 of 39 tasks can be done. The 15 that cannot are mostly missing features (#17, #18,
#20, #69), not slow screens.

## Findings, ranked by cost to the user

1. **Batches are never checked or released (#17).** Every batch stays "Pending check", so no
   batch can be released (C8), no check can be recorded (T8), and "can we supply this strain"
   (V4) is always no. The deposit check (C2) is a button with no place for its results.
2. **Nothing says what waits (#69).** Home reads "Strains, storage and distribution will
   appear here." A curator needs two filtered deposit lists and still misses batches pending
   check and vials to destroy, which exist only per strain (C3, T10). Every one of the 39
   tasks starts with the same two clicks and page loads, Labs and then the lab, about 7 s of a
   person's time each.
3. **Nothing leaves the collection (#18).** No request, approval, reservation or shipment:
   C9 and T9 stop. The vial state dialog offers neither reserved nor distributed.
4. **A strain has no history, and its page is one long column (#19, #72).** A risk group
   lowered from 2 to 1 leaves no trace on the page (C6). Passages, batches, moves and state
   changes have no time line (V5). The strain with 23 vials is 3 screens long and the vial
   asked for in T2 sits on screen 3. The vial table loads one page of 200 and shows no more (#72),
   so a busy strain grows to many screens and still hides vials.
5. **Finding a known strain or vial takes four page loads (new).** A strain number or a vial
   label is the thing people hold, but the only search is the strain list inside a lab: Home,
   Labs, lab, strain list, strain (V1, T2, V3). No search by vial label outside the box page.
6. **Placing vials is one dialog per vial, chosen blind (new, #30).** Three freeze-dried
   ampoules cost 32 steps and about 86 s (T3): the box is picked again for each vial, row and
   column are picked without seeing which places are free, and a long box path is cut at the
   end, where the box name is. Preserving into a box (T1) lists every box of the lab with no
   free count. Scanning on the box page is the faster path (T4, 21 steps for 4 tubes) but
   after each placement the focus stays on the grid, so each scan needs a click back into the
   field.
7. **The terms of use are not on the strain (new).** They live on the deposit, which the strain
   page does not link: the curator copies the name and searches the deposits (C9, 8 page
   loads), and a viewer may not open deposits at all, so "may we use it for teaching" has no
   answer (V7).
8. **No growth conditions (new).** The strain and its passages have no medium, temperature or
   atmosphere (V9), and a revival records none (T6). Both the OECD minimum data set
   (microorganism domain, Table 2) and WFCC 11.1 list growth conditions.
9. **No deaccession (#20).** The strain list filters by "Deaccessioned" but no screen sets
   it (C10).
10. **The receive form is the longest task (#30).** Receiving a deposit costs 42 steps and
    about 95 s (C1): 16 of its 32 clicks open and choose the eight use selects one by one,
    with no preset from the MTA.

Further findings, smaller:
- A storage unit does not say what it holds (new): the failing freezer's page lists its rack
  but no vial count or strain (C7), and the box grid shows labels without the organism (V6).
  After moving a rack the page stays on the rack, and the way back to the freezer is the tree.
- Managers have no oversight screens (new): no figures for the lab (M7), no screen of
  location reveals although each is recorded (M8), no list of permits or their expiry outside
  the Django admin (M9).
- The strain search reads number, name as received and taxon only (new): no isolation source,
  country or depositor (V8, C5).
- Adding a member needs the exact username of an existing account (M1).
- The deposit status filter applies at once and the Search button submits the filters as they
  were rendered: pressing Search right after choosing a status, before the list reloads, drops
  the status (seen in 5 of 6 fast tries, not at human speed) (new, small).
- The dialogs behaved the same everywhere (focus, Cancel, errors): nothing found for #67.

## Proposed issues
- Find a strain or a vial from anywhere: accession number, vial label or barcode, from the
  top bar.
- Place several vials in one step, with the free positions shown and the box remembered.
- Show the active terms of use on the strain page, to every role that sees the strain.
- Growth conditions on the strain and the passage: medium, temperature, atmosphere.
- What a storage unit holds: vial and strain counts per freezer, rack and box, and the
  organism in the box grid.
- Lab figures for managers: strains, vials, batches waiting, free places.
- Permits screen for managers: list, validity, expiry ahead.
- Reveal log for managers: who revealed which regulated vial, when.
- Strain search by isolation source, country and depositor.
- Deposit filters: keep the chosen status when Search is pressed before the list reloads.
